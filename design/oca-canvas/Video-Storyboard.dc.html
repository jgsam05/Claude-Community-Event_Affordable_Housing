Structure

Problem, then audience, then product, then walkthrough, then sign-off. This is a common shape for short product videos. Keep one idea per scene.

Look and sound

Use OCA colors and type only. Soft ease-out motion, no flashy transitions. Calm instrumental music under a clear voice, kept low in the demo half.

Before filming

Scenes 4 to 6 should be a real screen recording of the working app with real data. The sample data in these designs is a placeholder.
